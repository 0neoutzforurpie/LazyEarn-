# AdPulse Pay — PayPal Authenticator Login, Revenue-Capped Viewer Rewards & $10+ Cash Out Window

This update refines **AdPulse Pay** into a consumer-facing ad-watching rewards platform gated by a **PayPal Authenticator Login** before site entry, hides all internal website revenue disclosures from the user interface, enforces a **strict mathematical cap so interactive bonuses never exceed the ad's revenue**, and introduces an interactive **Cash Out Window** with a custom withdrawal amount input and a `$10.00` minimum disclaimer.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Review the confirmed product and UX updates below before we implement the changes.

- **Confirmed Decision 1 — Mandatory PayPal Authenticator Sign-In Before Using the Site**: Users must authenticate via the **PayPal Authenticator Login** screen before accessing the ad streaming studio, analytics, or payout ledger. The login flow supports both PayPal OAuth 2.0 popup authentication and an interactive PayPal Email + 6-Digit Authenticator Verification step.
- **Confirmed Decision 2 — Strict Cap on Interactive Bonus (Viewer Never Earns More Than Ad Revenue)**:
  - On the server, every ad has a total advertiser revenue value (`grossCpvUsd`, hidden from the viewer).
  - A viewer's **Base Reward** is set to **50%** of the ad's revenue, and answering the **Interactive Bonus Prompt** applies a modest boost (`1.20x`–`1.35x`, bringing the viewer's total share to at most **60%–67.5%** of the ad's revenue).
  - The backend enforces a hard ceiling (`netEarnedUsd = Math.min(grossRevenueUsd * 0.70, viewerBaseUsd * multiplierApplied)`) so a viewer's total payout (base + interactive bonus) can **never** exceed 70% of the ad's gross revenue—guaranteeing that the website always retains at least 30% net profit on every completed viewing session.
- **Confirmed Decision 3 — Hide All Website/Platform Earnings Information from Users**: Users will never see internal site revenue, platform treasury cuts, gross advertiser spend, or merchant API key metadata. Every screen (Video Player, Queue, Analytics, Payouts, and Earnings History) displays strictly the **user's own personal earnings**.
- **Confirmed Decision 4 — Interactive "Cash Out" Modal Window with $10.00 Minimum Disclaimer**: Clicking the **Cash Out** button opens a dedicated withdrawal modal asking *"How much do you want to withdraw?"* with a numeric input box where the user enters their chosen amount and a visible disclaimer stating that the minimum withdrawal amount is **$10.00 USD**.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Provides a secure, PayPal-authenticated video rewards portal where signed-in users watch commercial queues with uninterrupted autoplay, unlock bounded interactive bonuses that stay strictly within the ad's revenue budget, and withdraw any chosen amount ($10.00 minimum) directly to their authenticated PayPal account.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Viewers signing in with their PayPal account to watch commercial streams and cash out personal earnings.
- **Key Value**: Frictionless PayPal account binding at sign-in, sustainable unit economics (viewer base + bonus is strictly capped below total ad revenue), clean viewer-only earnings transparency, and a straightforward Cash Out popup window.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows
1. **PayPal Authenticator Gate (Before Site Entry)**:
   - Unauthenticated visitors see a sleek dark-mode **Sign In with PayPal Authenticator** portal before entering the site.
   - Users enter their PayPal email and complete the 6-digit PayPal Authenticator verification step (or launch the PayPal OAuth 2.0 popup flow) to establish an authenticated session.
2. **Viewer-Only Earnings & Bounded Interactive Bonus**:
   - **Ad Video Player & Autoplay Queue**: Shows only the viewer's personal reward per ad (e.g., `Base Reward: $0.50 · With 1.25x Bonus: $0.62 USD`) and watched percentage (`0%–100%`). All mentions of "Gross CPV", "Website Treasury", "Platform Fee", and "Merchant Key ID" are completely hidden from the user.
   - **Revenue Cap Safeguard**: Both the preview calculation and the backend `/api/sessions/complete` route clamp `(viewerBaseUsd + bonusUsd)` so it never exceeds 70% of the ad's underlying revenue (`grossRevenueUsd`), ensuring the interactive bonus never causes the user to earn more than the revenue generated by the ad.
3. **Cash Out Modal Window ("How much do you want to withdraw?")**:
   - Clicking the **Cash Out** button in the top navigation bar or studio opens a focused modal window titled **"How much do you want to withdraw?"**
   - Displays the user's current **Available Balance** and their authenticated PayPal account email.
   - Provides a custom amount input box where the user types the exact dollar amount they intend to withdraw.
   - Displays a clear disclaimer directly below the input box:
     > *"Disclaimer: The minimum amount you can withdraw is $10.00 USD."*
   - Validates that the entered amount is at least `$10.00` and does not exceed their available balance, and immediately processes the withdrawal to their logged-in PayPal account.

### Revenue Cap Formula (`server.ts`)
- For each ad campaign with internal advertiser revenue $R_{\text{ad}}$ (`grossCpvUsd`):
  - $\text{Viewer Base Reward} = 0.50 \times R_{\text{ad}}$
  - $\text{Interactive Multiplier} \in [1.20, 1.35]$
  - $\text{Uncapped Viewer Reward} = \text{Viewer Base Reward} \times \text{Multiplier}$
  - $\text{Final Viewer Reward} = \min\left(0.70 \times R_{\text{ad}}, \text{Uncapped Viewer Reward}\right)$
  - $\text{Website Net Retained Revenue} = R_{\text{ad}} - \text{Final Viewer Reward} \ge 0.30 \times R_{\text{ad}} > 0$
- This guarantees mathematically that a user can **never** earn more than (or even 100% of) the revenue for the ad they are watching.
